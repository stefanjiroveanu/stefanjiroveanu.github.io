Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how our home design content generation service collects, uses, and handles your information.

1. Information We Collect

When you connect your TikTok account, we collect:

  • TikTok account identifiers (username, user ID)
  • OAuth access tokens to post content on your behalf
  • Room photos you upload for design generation

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information solely to generate AI home design images and publish content to your TikTok account. We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

3. Data Storage

Access tokens are stored locally on your own infrastructure. Images are stored temporarily and deleted after posting.

4. Third-Party Services

  • TikTok API - to publish content to your account
  • OpenRouter / Flux AI - to generate design images

5. Your Rights

You may revoke access at any time via TikTok Settings → Security → Manage app permissions.
